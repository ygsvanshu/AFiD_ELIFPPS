# `*grid.out`

The set of ascii files `xgrid.out`, `ygrid.out`, and `zgrid.out` consist of information regarding the grid coordinates. For further information, refer to [spatial discretization](../../numerics/spatial-discretization.md).

The columns for `xgrid.out` are explained below. 

1.  `i`
2.  `xc(i)`
3.  `xm(i)`
4.  `dxc(i)`
5.  `dxm(i)`
6.  `ixcm(i)`
7.  `ixcp(i)`
8.  `am1si(i)`
9.  `ac1si(i)`
10. `ap1si(i)`
11. `am1ci(i)`
12. `ac1ci(i)`
13. `ap1ci(i)`
14. `amphi(i)`
15. `acphi(i)`
16. `apphi(i)`
17. `ak1(i)`

The columns for `ygrid.out` are explained below. 

1.  `j`
2.  `yc(j)`
3.  `ym(j)`
4.  `dyc(j)`
5.  `dym(j)`
6.  `iycm(j)`
7.  `iycp(j)`
8.  `am2sj(j)`
9.  `ac2sj(j)`
10. `ap2sj(j)`
11. `am2cj(j)`
12. `ac2cj(j)`
13. `ap2cj(j)`
14. `amphj(j)`
15. `acphj(j)`
16. `apphj(j)`
17. `ak2(j)`

The columns for `zgrid.out` are explained below. 

1.  `k`
2.  `zc(k)`
3.  `zm(k)`
4.  `dzc(k)`
5.  `dzm(k)`
6.  `izcm(k)`
7.  `izcp(k)`
8.  `am3sk(k)`
9.  `ac3sk(k)`
10. `ap3sk(k)`
11. `am3ck(k)`
12. `ac3ck(k)`
13. `ap3ck(k)`
14. `amphk(k)`
15. `acphk(k)`
16. `apphk(k)`
17. `ak3(k)`
